# Survivor.io optimizer source policy and scope

## Authority order

1. The user-supplied sIO Tools runtime bundle is the primary formula and structured-data authority.
2. The user's Discord formulas, `optimizer_source_reference.pdf`, and `source_database_map.pdf` are primary mechanic/data authorities.
3. YouTube, Reddit, Discord posts, Japanese/Chinese guides, Bilibili, Douyin, Tieba and other community sources are discovery and corroboration sources. A mechanic found there may be added when it matches sIO or the user-supplied Bible. Otherwise it remains `unknown`.
4. Unknown is acceptable. A fabricated score, cost, condition, uptime or legal transition is not.

## Supported mode

The optimizer supports **Clan Expedition damage only**.

It must not score or train on Ender's Echo, Lunar Mine Expedition, Path of Trials, chapters, Operation Retreat, Zone Operation, Survivor Showdown or event-only combat modifiers. Those fields may remain in raw source data, but they cannot alter the Clan Expedition champion.

## Exact scoring gate

A recommendation is scoreable only when the optimizer can:

1. Prove the action is legal.
2. Build the complete after-state.
3. Calculate before and after damage through the sIO Clan Expedition formula.
4. Produce a balanced resource/refund ledger.
5. Show every assumption or unsupported formula stage.

Rarity points, generic breakpoints and sync-rate deltas are not damage.
